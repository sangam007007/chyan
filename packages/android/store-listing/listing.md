## English (default language)

**App name** (max 30): `Chayan: Sarkari Bharti Prep`

**Short description** (max 80): `Govt exam prep: mock tests, PYQ, daily plan and AI analysis. Hindi + English`

**Full description** (max 4000):

Chayan is a preparation app for government recruitment exams. Study topic by topic, practise with tests, see exactly where you are weak, and keep going every day.

WHAT YOU GET
• Guided study path: every subject is split into topics with parts and a practice test after each
• Topic tests, subject full tests, full-length mock tests and previous-year papers (PYQ)
• Daily plan and daily challenge to build a habit
• AI-style analysis: spots weak topics, shows where to work harder, where you keep failing, and how close you are to exam-ready
• Compare with other students: average marks, how others answered each question, and your rank
• Streaks, XP levels and 60 badges to keep you motivated
• Discussion with other students for every test, and a community for your exam
• Hindi and English, switch any time

EXAMS
UP Police Constable, UP Police SI, SSC GD Constable, SSC CPO, Delhi Police Constable, Bihar Police Constable and more. New exams are added regularly.

FREE TRIAL
Start with a 3-day free trial with everything unlocked. After that, continue with a plan.

Sign in with your phone number (OTP) or Google. No password to remember.
